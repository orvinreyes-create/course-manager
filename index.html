Shadow Round

Play the round in your head before you play it for real.

  1. RehearsePlay your course the night before. Your handicap sets how the ball really flies — no hero swings.
  2. DecidePick a club, tap a target. The dots show where 250 of your swings would land. Aim where the trouble isn't.
  3. LearnAfter every shot the caddie tells you the smarter play and why. Take 2–3 rules to the real course.
  4. AcceptYour goal is your personal target, not par. Bogeys on hard holes are part of the plan.
